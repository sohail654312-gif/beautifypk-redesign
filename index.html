FAQ

Questions patients often need answered first.

Do I need to know which procedure I want before booking?+

No. You can start with the concern and use the consultation to compare suitable options.

What happens during a consultation?+

During your consultation, we review your concern, health, medicines and previous treatment, examine the relevant area, then discuss suitable options, likely outcomes, limitations, recovery, risks, aftercare and cost.

How much is the aesthetic consultation?+

The aesthetic consultation fee is PKR 3,000. Please confirm the fee when booking.

Are results the same for everyone?+

No. Anatomy, health, treatment, healing and aftercare affect outcomes, so results cannot be guaranteed.

How long does recovery take?+

Recovery depends on the exact procedure, health, work, healing and whether procedures are combined.
